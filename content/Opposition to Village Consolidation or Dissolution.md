Shout Out Saugerties recently convened a [meeting](https://www.shoutoutsaugerties.org/all-events/2026/7/13/balanced-growth-community-conversation-reorg-of-local-government) to discussion the consolidation or dissolution of the Village of Saugerties government. 

The Overlook covered the meeting in their article, [Saugerties Officials Weigh Combining Town and Village Governments](https://theoverlooknews.com/saugerties-officials-weigh-combining-town-and-village-governments/).

I sent (versions of) this note to Village's elected Mayor and Trustees to ensure they had at least one written opinion on the idea of consolidating our Village away. I encourage others to do the same. 

Long live the Village!

-----

Mayor Murphy,

I attended the information session on municipality consolidation at the Orpheum last week and appreciated your perspective on a potential consolidation.

I am a relative newcomer to the Village, having moved here in 2021, but I am quickly building a lasting life here. Since 2021 my wife and I have gotten married and had two children that we are excited to raise in Saugerties. When our second child arrived, we outgrew our home and intentionally moved just a half mile up the road so we could stay in the Village. We wanted to make sure our kids got to experience having such a wonderful community right outside their door, and they're now well known up and down Main and Partition streets.

I am extremely opposed to consolidation or dissolution of the Village into the Town, and frankly a little frustrated that we're spending time as a community talking about it. If so many folks are missing dinners with their families to think about the Village I'd much rather focus that energy on ways we can further improve our community, not defend against its dissolution.

From what I can see, the upside is extremely limited:

* The $1 million / year incentive would not make a material difference in the tax burden of Saugerties residents. It represents a 1-2% reduction in the total tax burden of Saugerties residents when compared to the appropriate denominator of \~$60M.  
  * Schools \- \~$41M \- $48M total, 85% of that is paid by Saugerties ([source](https://resources.finalsite.net/images/v1777390239/saugertiesk12nyus/bygwktu8k6a5lfsfuejs/2026-2027BudgetStatement.pdf))  
  * Town \- $13.5M ([source](https://www.dailyfreeman.com/2025/11/14/proposed-saugerties-town-budget-totals-17-71-million/))  
  * Village \- $3M \- ([source](https://www.dailyfreeman.com/2026/04/25/saugerties-village-board-will-vote-on-revised-proposed-budget/))  
  * Library \- $800k \- ([source](https://www.dailyfreeman.com/2026/06/30/saugerties-library-budget-drafted-at-886006-for-2027/))  
* We've already consolidated the police forces, so there is little room left to consolidate in a meaningful way. The bulk of our costs seem to be direct and indirect labor costs, and combining the Town and Village wouldn't mean we need any fewer guys out plowing the roads or inspecting our buildings.  
* Fred mentioned needing to come to the public for funding for new buildings for both the Town and Village highway departments. This is a one-time cost, and while unfortunate, wouldn't represent meaningful ongoing savings. We could likely find a solution for a consolidated building even as two separate municipalities, either via some sort of co-ownership or one building it and leasing space to the other.

However, the risks are significant:

* The biggest risk, for me, is that Villagers' voices get washed out when diluted by the Town. We've chosen to live in a small, walkable community for a reason, and should not expect our priorities to align with folks who choose to live further out in the suburbs or the woods. This isn't a value judgement, just a recognition that we cannot expect votes to go in our favor if we're only 20% of the population. A guaranteed "Village" town board seat would not solve this issue and we'd be at risk of losing our funding priorities, zoning, and downtown core if the Town residents favor a different direction.  
* I do not have a great understanding of the mechanics here, but I believe the Village owns the primary water source for much of the Town. It would be disadvantageous for us to lose that ownership, and I am concerned that if/when Winston Farm goes through we would struggle to make sure our residents have priority access to our water source over a new development. We are already straining to supply the current users of that water source.  
* We have stellar services in the Village; I believe this is both because we choose to fund them and we have a great team here. There has been plenty written about how the consolidation is impacting folks down in New Paltz, and labor is currently being asked to just trust that they will be taken care of, despite even the New Paltz Supervisor [disagreeing](https://hudsonvalleyone.com/2026/06/23/village-of-new-paltz-employees-voice-concerns-over-consolidation-in-a-tense-public-meeting/) with how departments would be combined in their latest proposal.  
* Further pursuit of dissolution or consolidation is likely to fuel senseless tensions between the Town and Village, and between many in the Village and the Town/Village governments. The majority of these proposals in NY go nowhere, but manage to burn resources and good will in the process.

I know many families with young children who feel similarly about the risks to Village life, and will encourage them to share their opinions as well. I know you are a strong proponent of the Village and recognize how special of a place it is. The Village has served Saugerties well and should be preserved for the next generation growing up here, not dissolved for immaterial (and theoretical) tax savings.

I'd love to see us instead focus on ways we can continue to strengthen, improve, and grow the Village. Ellenville just won a [$4.5M NY Forward grant](https://hudsonvalleyone.com/2026/07/15/a-4-5-million-state-grant-will-reshape-housing-and-downtown-development-in-ellenville/) to revitalize its downtown, and we just proved that Saugerties can win grants with [$600k coming for our splashpad](https://theoverlooknews.com/community-foundation-of-saugerties-gets-605000-grant-for-splash-pad/). Let's protect the Village and help it continue to grow by taking advantage of more grants like these\!

I'm glad to discuss further and help out in any way I can.

Best,   
Matt Vivier
